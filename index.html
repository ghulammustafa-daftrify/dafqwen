How a file moves

From scattered pages
to one dossier.

01

Receive

Your stack arrives exactly as it exists — no renaming, no sorting, no tidying required. Through the form, email, or WhatsApp.

02

Triage

Every page is read and sorted into a working order. Duplicates, gaps, and unreadable pages are flagged before anything else happens.

03

Reconcile

Values are extracted and cross-checked against the source documents. Variances get itemized in an exception register — nothing glossed over.

04

Dossier

One structured, indexed package comes back: clean sheets, reconciled tables, and the exception register up front.

Services

What the desk takes on.

Three kinds of work. If your stack doesn't fit any of them, say so in the intake form — you'll get a straight answer.

01

Triage & organization

Messy, mixed-source stacks get read page by page and rebuilt into a clean working order — indexed, de-duplicated, with gaps flagged up front.

02

Reconciliation & cross-checks

Figures pulled from PDFs, scans, and spreadsheets are checked against each other and against the source. Variances are itemized, not buried.

03

Dossier assembly

The finished file: structured sheets, reconciled tables, and an exception register — one package, ready to hand to whoever needs it next.

The desk

One desk. One pair of hands.

Daftrify is a one-person document operations desk in Faisalabad, Pakistan. No ticket queues, no account managers relaying messages — the person you write to is the person who opens your file.

It started from a simple observation: the messiest part of any submission isn't the work itself, it's the paperwork around it. Daftrify exists to take that part off your table, properly.

FAQ

Asked, answered.

What kinds of documents do you handle?+

Multi-page PDFs, scanned contracts, invoices, financial ledgers, Excel tables, technical drawings, forms and correspondence. If it lives on paper or on a drive, we can work with it.

How fast is the turnaround?+

Turnaround depends on the stack. Larger or unusually tangled sets get a firm time estimate before we begin — agreed up front, not discovered later.

How do I send my files?+

Through the intake form below, by email at contact@daftrify.info, or over WhatsApp. No account, no login, no portal to learn.

How is pricing decided?+

Every stack is different, so pricing is per project. Send your files and you get a clear quote before anything starts.

Is my information kept confidential?+

Yes. Files are handled confidentially at the desk and used only for your file.

Do you use AI to process documents?+

Software assists with extraction, and output is checked back against the source before it leaves the desk.
