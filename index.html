Principles

The operating rules, in plain words.

Source first. No value enters the working file unless it can be traced to a document you actually hold. If it cannot be traced, it is reported as a gap — never filled in.

Exceptions are visible. Problems are listed at the front of the package, source attached to each one. Nothing is buried where nobody reads.

Verify against the record. A person reads each extracted value back against the original before anything is marked verified.

No invented facts. No fake proof. No fabricated records, no borrowed logos, no numbers we cannot stand behind.

What we don't handle

No legal, financial, medical or immigration advice. No representation before any authority. No outcome claims — you keep the decisions, we do the document work.

Common questions

Anything document-heavy where accuracy is non-negotiable: multi-page PDF stacks, dense financial ledgers, contractor invoices, Excel tables, handwritten receipts, architectural drawing sets, tax bundles, compliance files, and recurring back-office documentation. You can send the material exactly as it exists — no renaming, no sorting, and no tidying required.

Your files pass through a four-stage engine: multi-format triage intake, multimodal OCR data extraction, and bi-directional relational cross-reconciliation. Before anything leaves the desk, Ghulam Mustafa conducts a human verification pass — reading each extracted value back against the original source character by character. We never hallucinate or invent data.

Every engagement operates under strict mutual NDA standards with end-to-end 256-bit encryption. Your material is used solely to execute your specific file audit — never published, resold, or used for AI model training. Once delivery is accepted, files are completely purged from our workspace upon your confirmation.

Standard initial triage readouts are returned within 24 hours. Pricing is scoped transparently by the actual set: volume of sheets, source material clarity, cross-reference density, and deadline urgency. There are no surprise retainers or generic inflated cards — you receive an honest fixed quotation after we inspect the stack.

You receive one structured, indexed package: an upfront Exception Register itemizing every variance or missing gap, reconciled cross-tables, highlighted PDF callouts, and clean export sheets. Everything is audit-ready and pre-formatted for direct submission to legal counsel, banks, or regulatory authorities.
